Local harness for flow-bulk-downloader.user.js

Fakes Tampermonkey's GM_* APIs, Flow's WIZ_global_data, a batchexecute backend (listing, media, credits, upscale) and a project grid with Flow-like hover menus (Download → 1K Original / 2K Upscaled / 4K Upscaled). Upscale #2 fails with a toast and every upscale after the 3rd successful one hits a fake daily limit (add &lim=vague to word it as a plain failure, &lim=grey to grey out the upscale entries instead, or &lim=none for no failures or limit; &plan=4k unlocks 4K). Local testing only.
